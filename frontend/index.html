Guide to Badges & Symbols

Source Reliability (Tier)
institutional
Regulatory agencies and official databases (EMA, FDA, WHO, DailyMed, RxNav, ChEMBL).
curated
Peer-reviewed literature and clinical registries (PubMed, Europe PMC, ClinicalTrials.gov, Open Targets).
general
Preprints and open non-peer-reviewed sources (bioRxiv, medRxiv, Wikipedia).
Matching & Relevance
extrapolated
Topically relevant source, but on a related population, condition, or formulation rather than the exact query (adjacent PICO).
75%
Direct topical relevance assessed against the clinical question.
Study Design (Evidence Hierarchy)
systematic_review / rct
Highest hierarchy: systematic reviews, meta-analyses, randomized controlled trials, guidelines.
cohort / observational
Intermediate hierarchy: observational, cohort, or case-control studies.
in_vitro / case_report
Preclinical evidence, case series, or experimental models.
Claim Strength (Overview)
strong
Direct, consistent, high-tier evidence.
moderate
Observational evidence or moderate consensus.
weak
Preliminary or conflicting evidence, or based solely on extrapolated sources.
